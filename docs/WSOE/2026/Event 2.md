## 📃 Format

- 💬 Entropy bidding format ("Chaostropy")
- 🃏 Standard 52-card deck plus 2 Jokers. Jokers count as 2 of every suit.
- 🖐️ 5-card hands
- ❗ Illegal allowed while >= 8 cards in play
- 👀 Players may reveal cards alongside a bid, shown in brackets in the bid column
- ⏭️ 2 passes each, plus 1 extra when down to 1 card
- ⚡ Super-challenge / Super-Illegal: the loser loses 2 cards and the winner gains 1. Not allowed with only 1 card.
- 📉 Losing player loses 1 card and starts the next round.
